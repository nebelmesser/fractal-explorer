The map

Each pixel starts an orbit at angle θ and momentum p, then repeatedly applies a kick controlled by K. Smooth colored layers are regular motion; broken and speckled layers are chaotic motion. Hue follows the orbit's average phase, while brightness shows how coherently it stays in one rotational layer. The opening K value is close to the famous transition where the last large invariant barrier breaks.

Navigation

Click or double-tap to zoom, drag to pan, and use the wheel, pinch, or +/− buttons. Right-click and reset view go back. Change K to move from nearly parallel invariant curves through mixed islands to widespread chaos. Accuracy controls how many kicks are sampled per pixel.
