The map

Each pixel runs the logistic rule x(next) = r × x × (1 − x). The growth value r follows the repeating rhythm AABAB, while the pixel position supplies A horizontally and B vertically. Gold means nearby starting values converge to a stable cycle (Lyapunov exponent λ < 0). Blue means they separate and the motion is chaotic (λ > 0). The black shoreline is the transition around λ = 0. Hover to read A, B, and λ.
