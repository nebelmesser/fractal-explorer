The map

Each point is a pendulum bob released from rest above a table with three magnets at the vertices of an equilateral triangle. Red, green, and blue are the three magnets: the pixel mixes those colors by how long the bob spent near each one. Hover to see one trajectory. Gravity restores the bob toward the origin; friction damps the motion; magnet strength, height above the table, and step size are in settings.

Navigation

Pan and zoom: click or double-tap, wheel, pinch, +/− buttons. Right-click and reset view go back. Nearby starts can end on different magnets — the boundaries are fractal.
